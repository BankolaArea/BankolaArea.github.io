Formula & Technical Specifications

1. Interpolation Formula

V = V₁ + (h − h₁) × (V₂ − V₁) ⁄ (h₂ − h₁)

Where [h₁, h₂] is the bounding dipstick interval (cm) from certified calibration data and [V₁, V₂] is the corresponding 200 L volume interval.

2. Tanker Floor Anchor

For readings between 0.0 cm and the first 200 L calibration mark, interpolation is strictly anchored at (0.0 cm, 0 L) to reflect floor curvature accurately.

3. Range & Boundary Handling

Readings below 0.0 cm are rejected as Invalid Negative. Readings exceeding certified maximum dipstick height are flagged as Overflow Risk.

4. Sensitivity & Capacity

Average sensitivity is ~35.7 L/cm across all 4 chambers, with full tanker capacity verified at exactly 20,000 Liters.
